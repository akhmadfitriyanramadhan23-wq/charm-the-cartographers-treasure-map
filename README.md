One picture: a treasure map of the Isle of Mischief, an island that does not exist, drawn by a cartographer who got lost twice. Eight labeled places — Whispering Dunes, Fort Snooze, Crab Court, The Grumpy Volcano, Ticklewood Forest, Giggle Brook, Mermaid's Rest, and Captain's Last Laugh, where the dashed route ends at a big red X — plus a compass rose, a sea monster, and a small ship that was told not to feed the kraken.

`tools/make-image.py` (Python 3 + PIL, fixed seed, fully procedural) renders `entry.png` (1024×1024, 211,261 bytes); `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies it to `/output/entry.png`. Entry for [the bounty on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ravendell-charm-the-cartographers-a-one-day-00854547).
